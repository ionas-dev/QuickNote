Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Short version: QuickNote keeps your notes on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no tracking and no analytics. The developer never receives your data.

Who is responsible

QuickNote is developed by Jonas Kaiser. For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/ionas-dev/QuickNote/issues.

Your notes

Your notes, tags and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. QuickNote has no servers and does not send your notes anywhere.

Like all app data, they are included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and your own devices, not by QuickNote.

Reminders

If you turn a note into a to-do, QuickNote asks for permission to access Apple Reminders. With your permission it creates the reminder, later reads and updates its title and completion state so the note stays in sync, and deletes it if you delete the to-do in QuickNote.

These reminders live in Apple's Reminders app and sync through your iCloud account if you use iCloud for Reminders. QuickNote only accesses reminders it created for your notes, and does not send them anywhere. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders.

QuickNote does not access your calendar.

No tracking, analytics or ads

QuickNote contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party services. It does not collect usage data, device identifiers or location.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This is controlled by Apple and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all notes, tags and settings stored by QuickNote on your device. Reminders you created remain in the Reminders app until you delete them there.

Children

QuickNote does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If QuickNote's handling of data changes, for example with a future sync feature, this page will be updated before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
