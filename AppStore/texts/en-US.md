## Description
Write now, sort later. QuickNote opens straight to today's note, so a thought is saved before it's gone — no folders, no titles, no setup.

When you have a moment, give your notes structure:

• One note per day — open the app and start typing
• Tags with emoji and colors to sort thoughts by topic
• Turn any line into a to-do and tick it off
• Send blocks to Reminders
• Move a thought to the day it belongs to
• See everything you haven't sorted yet in one place
• Find past notes by date in the month calendar
• Make it yours: light or dark, sans or serif, cards or plain lists

Your notes are stored on your device.
